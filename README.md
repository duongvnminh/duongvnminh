## About

PhD candidate at the University of Washington and intern at **Pacific Northwest National Laboratory**, contributing to the **Battery 500 Project** — a DOE initiative targeting 500 Wh/kg energy density. Research sits at the intersection of electrochemistry and machine learning: building data-driven frameworks to understand, predict, and design electrolytes for rechargeable lithium metal batteries.
